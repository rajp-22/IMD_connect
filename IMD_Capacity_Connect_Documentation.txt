1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW & MISSION
================================================================================
IMD MeghSetu is an institutional, role-based competency intelligence 
and capacity-building digital platform specifically architected for the 
India Meteorological Department (IMD), Ministry of Earth Sciences (MoES).

The platform transforms institutional training from ad-hoc seminar attendance 
into a deterministic, transparent, and data-backed competency management 
ecosystem. It serves three vital user cadres:
1. Operational Trainees & Forecasters (upskilling, diagnostic assessments, certifications)
2. Certified Faculty & Trainers (curriculum authoring, progress supervision, matching)
3. Directorate & Training Administrators (nationwide station analytics, skill heatmaps,
   cadre sanctioning, impact assessment)

Key Value Pillars:
- Zero-Hallucination Technical Grounding: Context-bound pedagogical AI.
- Deterministic Evaluation: Objective pre-test vs. post-test quantifiable gains.
- Transparent Skill Gap Diagnostics: Explaining exactly WHY a course is recommended.
- Authentic Digital Credentials: Verifiable certificates with QR code verification.
- Official Government Visual Language: Clean, dignified, institutional aesthetic.

4. CORE PLATFORM FEATURES (DETAILED BREAKDOWN)
================================================================================

FEATURE 1: Competency Intelligence Engine & Scoring
- Tracks 10 core meteorological domains: Weather Forecasting, Meteorology, Data Analysis,
  Python, Satellite Meteorology, Climate Science, Numerical Weather Prediction (NWP),
  Radar Meteorology, Tropical Meteorology, Instrumentation.
- Scores range from 0 to 100% across four capability levels: Beginner, Intermediate, 
  Advanced, and Expert.
- Objective algorithmic scoring tied to pre-tests, lesson completions, and final exams.

FEATURE 2: Role-Based Competency Matrix Comparison
- Cadre benchmark templates (e.g., Lead Forecaster [WF-CORE], Satellite Specialist [SAT-SPEC],
  Climate Data Analyst [CLI-SPEC], Radar Meteorologist [RAD-SPEC]).
- Dynamic comparison between user's current score and official cadre requirement.
- Automatic Role Readiness metric calculation (e.g., 68% role readiness for Forecaster).

FEATURE 3: Smart Skill Gap Visualizer & Remedial Recommendations
- Formula: Skill Gap = Required Standard - Current Score.
- Status categorization: Critical Gap (>30%), Moderate Gap (15-30%), Satisfied (<15%).
- Direct one-click remedial course call-to-action [Start Learning] targeted specifically 
  to address the deficiency.

FEATURE 4: Transparent "Why" Recommendation Engine
- Never recommends a course blindly.
- Displays transparent justification chips, e.g.:
  * "Addresses your Weather Forecasting skill gap (35%)"
  * "Direct prerequisite for Lead Forecaster role"
  * "Matches your interest in Computational GIS"
- Displays match scores (e.g., 94% Match) and prerequisite fulfillment status.

FEATURE 5: Multi-Modal Learning Path & Interactive Timeline
- Connects curriculum milestones in a clear sequential journey.
- Visual state indicators: Completed (Green Checkmark), Active (Pulsing Blue Ring),
  Upcoming (Slate Ring), and Locked (Padlock Icon with prerequisite requirements).
- Prevents skipping foundational atmospheric physics before advanced nowcasting.

FEATURE 6 & 7: Baseline Diagnostic Pre-Assessments & Post-Test Certification Exams
- Pre-Assessment: Taken before course unlock to capture incoming baseline knowledge.
- Post-Assessment: Taken after completing all syllabus modules.
- Timed examination engine with question steppers, multiple choice, and immediate
  score breakdown with physical scientific explanations.
- Automatic calculation of Net Point Gain (e.g., Pre 42% -> Post 81% = +39 pts gain).

FEATURE 8: Curriculum & Course Player Studio
- Recommended structured layout:
  Course Header -> Description -> Progress Gauge -> Curriculum Syllabus -> 
  Downloadable Learning Resources -> Evaluation Assessments -> Trainer Credentials.
- Supports multi-format content: Articles, Interactive Text, Video Lectures, and PDF Guides.
- Mark as Complete tracking with auto-updating progress bar.

FEATURE 9: Real-time In-Document Q&A Bar
- Embedded directly within each lesson module.
- Allows trainees to query specific questions against the active lesson syllabus text.
- Grounded strictly in the provided scientific text without hallucinations.

FEATURE 10: Departmental Competency Coverage Heatmap (Admin)
- Matrix visualization of 5 key IMD departments (NWFC, RMC Mumbai, Satellite Division,
  AWS Network, Climatology Pune) across 5 core technical competencies.
- Color-coded cell thresholds: Green (>=70% Satisfied), Amber (55-69% Moderate Gap),
  Red (<55% Critical Gap).
- Interactive drill-down modal on cell click showing staff headcount, average score,
  required score, gap breakdown, and remedial course deployment button.

FEATURE 11: Department Training Insights & Priority Skill Gaps
- Automated institutional aggregation listing top 5 nationwide skill gaps.
- Displays affected personnel headcount and course demand indexes.
- Allows administrators to assign remedial training cohorts nationwide.

FEATURE 12: Training Impact Analytics (Pre vs. Post Gains)
- Quantifiable ROI metrics on training programs across 20 regional meteorological stations.
- Key KPIs: Average Improvement (+23.6 pts), Course Completion Rate (84.2%), Total Learning
  Hours (1,840 hrs), and Course Engagement Rate (91.8%).
- Recharts visual bar chart comparing Pre-Test vs. Post-Test outcomes per course.

FEATURE 13: Deterministic Trainer Matching Tool
- Algorithmic matching of course prerequisites with verified faculty competency scores.
- Selectable required competencies (Meteorology, Radar, Python, Satellite, etc.).
- Ranks available trainers with match percentage (e.g., 88% Match), experience years,
  official star rating, and competency checklist breakdown.

FEATURE 14: Centralized Digital Certificate Registry
- Secure registry of all certificates issued across the department.
- Cryptographic unique identifier: e.g., IMD-CERT-2026-000128.
- Contains trainee name, cadre designation, curriculum, trainer, exam score, and issue date.

FEATURE 15: Public QR Code Verification Portal (/verify/[id])
- Independent verification route for credential authenticity.
- Scanned via QR code or manual identifier input.
- Displays official verification banner with Government of India and MoES header,
  confirming official record on the IMD Central Capacity Verification Ledger.

FEATURE 16: Competency Passport (/trainee/passport)
- Dignified official credential document for cadre officers.
- Summarizes total training hours, courses completed, examination average, verified
  competencies, registered professional certificates, and institutional milestones.

FEATURE 17: Interactive Knowledge & Competency Graph
- SVG-rendered interactive graph linking core meteorological domains, syllabus topics,
  and competency prerequisites.
- Node inspection reveals connected courses, cadre requirements, and difficulty levels.

FEATURE 18: Integrated MeghSetu AI Pedagogical Console
- Institutional assistant with context awareness (active course/lesson context indicator).
- One-click prompt chips:
  * "Explain this concept"
  * "Summarize this lesson"
  * "Create practice questions"
  * "What should I learn next?"
- Authorized citation blocks and clean pedagogical formatting.

FEATURE 19: Global Grouped Knowledge Search Modal
- Accessible via global shortcut (Ctrl+K / Cmd+K) or header search bar.
- Groups search results into distinct categorized sections:
  * COURSES
  * LEARNING MATERIAL & HANDBOOKS
  * LESSONS & SYLLABUS TOPICS
  * COMPETENCIES & BENCHMARKS
  * ASSESSMENTS & EXAMS

FEATURE 20: National Training Calendar & Scheduling
- Integrated view of live Webex training sessions, assessment deadlines, course batch
  starts, national workshops, and certificate validity expiries.
- Filterable by event category with direct session links.

FEATURE 21: Early Warning & Support Signal Engine
- Proactively flags trainees with low pre-test scores (<50%) or stagnant module progress.
- Alerts both the trainee and the assigned trainer with remedial recommendations.

FEATURE 22: Gamification & Institutional Qualification Badges
- Replaces informal gaming badges with dignified professional qualification milestones:
  * "Synoptic Analysis Specialist"
  * "Radar Echo Interpreter"
  * "AWS Maintenance Certification"
  * "Numerical Model Analyst"

5. END-TO-END OPERATIONAL WORKFLOWS
================================================================================

WORKFLOW 1: TRAINEE LEARNING & CERTIFICATION WORKFLOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Sign In & Identity Selection:
   - Trainee signs in via login page or selects "Trainee Demo" from Demo Switcher.
   - Redirected to Trainee Dashboard (/trainee/dashboard).

2. Dashboard Triage:
   - Trainee inspects the prioritized dashboard:
     * Card 1: Continue Learning (resumes Weather Forecasting Fundamentals).
     * Card 2: Recommended Courses (reviews transparent match reasons).
     * Card 3: Skill Gaps (observes 35% gap in Weather Forecasting).
     * Card 4: Learning Path Timeline (observes current node in path).

3. Taking the Diagnostic Pre-Assessment:
   - Trainee launches pre-test before starting course modules.
   - Answers 5 diagnostic MCQs within the timed runner.
   - Baseline diagnostic score (e.g., 42%) is recorded to user profile.

4. Studying Lessons & In-Document Q&A:
   - Enters Course Player (/courses/[id]).
   - Reads atmospheric thermodynamic principles and lesson notes.
   - Queries the Document Q&A Bar: "What is the adiabatic lapse rate?"
   - Receives grounded explanation extracted directly from lesson text.
   - Clicks "Mark Lesson as Completed" to advance syllabus progress bar.

5. Final Certification Examination:
   - Upon completing all modules, trainee unlocks the final certification exam.
   - Completes evaluation questions within the timed assessment runner.
   - Scores 81% (passing benchmark: 70%).
   - Net gain of +39 points is computed and recorded.

6. Certificate Issuance & QR Verification:
   - Passing triggers celebratory verification dialog.
   - Digital Certificate is minted with unique ID (e.g., IMD-CERT-2026-000128).
   - Trainee can preview, print, or scan the verification QR code.
   - Credential is added to the Trainee's Competency Passport.


WORKFLOW 2: TRAINER / FACULTY CURRICULUM & SUPERVISION WORKFLOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Faculty Access:
   - Trainer logs in or switches to "Trainer Demo" (Dr. Rajesh Sharma).
   - Lands on Trainer Dashboard (/trainer/dashboard).

2. Reviewing Active Curricula:
   - Inspects authored courses with live enrolled headcount.
   - Reviews Trainee Progress roster to identify students falling behind.

3. Evaluating Training Outcomes:
   - Reviews pre-test vs. post-test evaluation metrics across batches.
   - Observes average class improvement from 45% baseline to 82% post-test.

4. Authoring a New Course (/trainer/courses/create):
   - Opens the 7-step Course Creation Studio:
     * Step 1: Course Info (Title, Category, Difficulty, Duration, Competency Domain).
     * Step 2: Modules Hierarchy (Add modules and assign order).
     * Step 3: Lessons & Lectures (Add lesson text, articles, slides, or video links).
     * Step 4: Upload Study Materials (Attach PDF manuals with safe storage fallback).
     * Step 5: Author MCQ Assessment (Set questions, options, correct answers, explanations).
     * Step 6: Preview Curriculum Summary.
     * Step 7: Confirm & Publish to MongoDB / In-Memory Store.

5. Competency Matching Assignment:
   - Trainer inspects Trainer Matching Tool (/trainer/trainer-matching).
   - Verifies their faculty profile rating (4.9/5.0) and domain competencies.


WORKFLOW 3: ADMINISTRATOR & DIRECTORATE GOVERNANCE WORKFLOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Executive Oversight:
   - Administrator logs in or switches to "Admin Demo" (Dr. M. Mohapatra).
   - Lands on Admin Dashboard (/admin/dashboard).

2. Reviewing Nationwide Statistics:
   - Inspects 6 key executive metrics: Total Users (30), Active Trainees (18),
     Certified Trainers (7), Courses Catalog (5), Enrollments (42), Certificates (14).
   - Analyzes monthly enrollment vs. completion bar trends and role donut charts.

3. Departmental Competency Coverage Analysis:
   - Navigates to Skill Heatmap (/admin/heatmap).
   - Identifies critical red cells (e.g., Satellite Division in Python: 52%).
   - Clicks cell to open drill-down modal showing 6 affected staff members.
   - Clicks "Deploy Remedial Course" to assign training intervention.

4. Quantifying Training ROI:
   - Opens Training Impact Analytics (/admin/training-impact).
   - Reviews net improvement (+23.6 pts) across 20 regional stations.
   - Exports/verifies course-by-course and department-by-department breakdowns.

5. Managing Personnel Sanctions & Role Approvals:
   - Reviews Pending Users Table (/admin/users).
   - Approves newly registered officers into Forecaster or Trainer cadres.


WORKFLOW 4: PUBLIC CREDENTIAL VERIFICATION WORKFLOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Verification Trigger:
   - Third-party agency, regional station, or officer scans QR code on certificate.
   - QR code resolves to: https://capacityconnect.imd.gov.in/verify/CC-2026-000128.

2. Ledger Query:
   - System queries MongoDB / in-memory store by verificationCode.
   - If found:
     * Displays green verified banner: "Certificate Verified Authentic ✓"
     * Shows Trainee Name, Certified Course, Trainer, Issue Date, Authority.
     * Displays IMD MoES official header strip and privacy-compliance notice.
   - If invalid code:
     * Displays clear verification failure state with retry assistance.
